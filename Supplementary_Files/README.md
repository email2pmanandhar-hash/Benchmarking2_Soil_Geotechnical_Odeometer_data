# Supplementary material for Studies A B and C

Read Supplementary_Guide_Studies_A_B_C.pdf first. SUBMISSION_FILE_LIST.csv lists the journal-facing components; FILE_INDEX.csv lists every submitted file and SHA-256. The three study folders contain canonical results, identifiers, final development-CV scores, code and available original provenance records. Cross_study contains the combined SHAP index and unrounded learner ranks.

Use results/summary_mean_sd.csv for retained summaries. Original per-seed metrics are supplied once as provenance/source_records/primary_metric_export_original.csv.gz. The reader verification/read_retained_metrics.py selects learner rows and applies the B/C empirical comparator replacements; it can export a complete retained per-seed CSV when required. Study B/C use their separate empirical OK/RK predictions and selected settings; superseded standalone heuristic comparator outputs are omitted. The gzip-compressed original primary metric receipt under provenance/source_records retains source bytes for hash checks and may contain superseded comparator rows. It is not the canonical comparison file. Original notebooks are execution/source evidence and may display historical tables or generic labels; use the canonical results for numerical reporting.

Study B's inherited StudyC filenames identify the shared engine, not the 106-record Study C population. The S2 folder and its split IDs define Study B (555 records/52 boreholes). The original SHAP/prediction PNGs and their provenance are retained once; duplicate manuscript renderings and unpresented force plots are omitted. Final learner CV score tables include all twenty candidates; intermediate initial/probe exports and duplicate selected/boundary tables are consolidated into the selected-settings and boundary-audit records.

Study B's empirical tuning, compatibility and locked-setting manifests and Study C's primary run/execution manifests are supplied in the respective provenance/original/ folders. Their source locations and unchanged hashes are recorded in provenance/SOURCE_RECORD_MAP.csv. Primary manifests preserve historical heuristic comparator settings; the separate empirical tuning manifests define the retained B/C OK/RK settings. 


Study C retains OKRK_OP_25Nov empirical results linked to StudyC_reconciled_25Nov primary records. Its solver uses variance-normalized semivariogram form, jitter 1e-10, and a 1000-evaluation fit limit without retries. The two supplied empirical source versions remain distinct and their exact-source qualification is preserved in DATA_AVAILABILITY.csv.
